Client guide

Know before you invest: four things every business should understand

A quick, jargon-free primer so you can make confident decisions — whether you work with me or not.

01

Your website is a salesperson

A good site explains your offer in seconds, loads fast on mobile, builds trust and gives one clear next step.

Check: can a stranger tell what you do within five seconds?
02

SEO vs ads

Ads bring visitors quickly but stop when the budget stops. SEO takes longer to build but keeps compounding.

Most businesses do best with a mix of both.
03

Leads need follow-up

Enquiries go cold fast. Automated replies and reminders make sure no lead is forgotten.

Reply quickly — speed often decides who wins the customer.
04

Social builds trust

Social media shows you're active and credible. Consistency matters more than going viral.

Pick one or two channels where your customers are, and do them well.
