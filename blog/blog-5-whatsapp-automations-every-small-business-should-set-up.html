5 WhatsApp Automations Every Small Business Should Set Up

Ayush · Founder, TechTapasvi12 Oct 20262 min read

You do not need a big budget. These five flows save hours and stop enquiries slipping away.

1. Instant greeting and away message

Reply immediately, even outside working hours, with a friendly message and what happens next.

2. Quick-reply FAQs

Save answers to common questions such as pricing, location and timings so you can reply with a tap.

3. Catalogue and order flow

Share products and capture the order details in a structured way instead of scattered chats.

4. Appointment and payment reminders

Automated reminders reduce no-shows and late payments without awkward follow-ups.

5. Follow-up after purchase

Send a thank-you, ask for a review and share a reorder reminder at the right time.

A word on permission

Only message people who have contacted you or opted in, and use approved templates for business-initiated messages.

Key takeaway

Start with one or two flows, measure the time saved and add more as you grow.
